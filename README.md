# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, product performance, regional performance, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

The project combines **Python/Pandas for data analysis** with an **interactive Power BI dashboard** to generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high- and low-performing products and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profit across regions and customer segments.
- Analyze the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python**
- **Pandas**
- **Power BI**
- **Matplotlib / Seaborn**

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains **10K+ retail sales records** with information about:

- Orders and customers
- Products and categories
- Sales and quantity
- Discounts
- Profit
- Regions and segments
- Order and shipping dates

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Data Cleaning
- Handled date formatting and conversion.
- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Validated data types and numerical fields.

### Exploratory Data Analysis
- Category and sub-category performance analysis.
- Regional and customer-segment analysis.
- Yearly and monthly sales and profit trends.
- Product-level profit analysis.
- Discount vs. profit analysis.
- Identification of high- and low-performing products.

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Sales & Profit by Category
- Sales & Profit by Region
- Yearly Sales & Profit Trend
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit
- Discount vs. Average Profit analysis
- Interactive Year, Region, Category, and Segment slicers
- Reset Filters functionality

## 💡 Key Insights

- Technology generated the highest overall sales and profit among the major categories.
- Tables was the weakest-performing sub-category and generated negative overall profit.
- Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower average profit.
- West region had the highest overall profit margin.
- Technology had the lowest average discount among the major categories.
- Several products showed significant negative profitability and require further review.
